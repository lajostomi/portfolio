WORK ABOUT CONTACT

Increasing interactions
on the MOME University Life Platform

A case study on the app and recommendations on its development based on real research data

2025

Research, UX

Consultant: Tamás Fogarasy

Context

MOME is a university of art and design in Budapest, dedicated to foster young talents from Central and Eastern Europe.

Application

The app does not offer any real value to the students for them to prefer it over the other communication channels of the university (e.g. email, instagram, posters, word of mouth)

*screenshot from the ULP app

Reality

The university caters to students with many recreational activities and opportunities, but these are often missed out on because of the flooded communication channels.

The main issues that I found were that:

synthesis

University life happens on too many channels (Email, Teams, Facebook, Instagram), making students anxious they are missing out.

The current platform is more of a directory of hyperlinks that kick users out to external browsers, breaking their flow.

Students feel like a lot of MOME’s resources could be integrated into the app. Let that be more information, or utility features.

Students default to pen and paper because digital tools fail to capture the nuance of in-person verbal announcements.

How might we unite and target informational channels so that MOME students get the right amount and relevant information to them?

How might we make important information that is only mentioned during classes in person trackable so that it is not lost?

How might we eleminate external links so that MOME students have an easier time navigating the platform while also keeping the important information they point towards?

How might we integrate more resources into the app without making it feel overwhelming?

I did a series of usability tests as time was scarce and I needed fast progress

method: R.I.T.E. + think aloud

Repeat 3 times

Test

I asked the students to complete the following tasks:

Task I.

“You see an interesting campus activity coming up next week.

How would you keep track of it so you don’t forget?”

Task II.

“Show me where you’d go to check the details of that activity you kept track of.”

Task III.

“You’re planning to eat lunch on campus today.

Check what food options are being served right now.”

Task IV.

“You’re looking to join an athletic club or stay active this semester.

How would you explore your options?”
