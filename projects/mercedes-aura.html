Mercedes Aura:
A tactile adventure 2046

Creating a unique in-car experience in collaboration with the Mercedes UX department.

2026

UX / UI, Vibe coding

Consultants: MOME: Péter Molnár, Viktor Horváth,
Mercedes AG: David Wilfinger, Celine Benachour

Team: Róza Parlagi, Vladimir Borovic, Uros Gavrilovic

Problem

In 2026 children's uncontrollable screentime gets into the way of creating real memories and experiences. Even in a road trip settings when it should be about the family bonding.

On the other hand humans are getting distanced from nature which plays an essential part in the development of human brain activity.

Solution

We created a multisensoral experience for the whole family based on childrens natural behaviour of touching and collecting things.

How was it when we were children?

We rummaged the forest, riverside and fields for little trinkets: pinecones, sticks, little rocks. Touching these tactile surfaces created essential neural networks in our brain. (Kolb,2012)

Collecting and revisiting objects

Having digital storage for all the items was necessary so children can hold onto them without physically filling the car up.

Alternative library view (vibecoded)

We played with different gallery view modes aswell.

One was a vibecoded infinite field with all the gathered objects, which could be dragged around and played with by the children.

This could open the possibility of changing with environment or seasons.

Prototyping
challenges

We had to come up with a smart workaround due to our unique screen shape.

Using projection mapping controlled by a hidden iPad we managed to create a seamless experience for our testers.

To sync the controls and the visuals we used a special vibe coded software.

Live demo and testing

Our consultants from MOME and Mercedes AG tried out our demo and reached a point of immersion which we considered a great success.

We were invited to showcase our project at Stuttgart Mercedes HQ in August, 2026.
